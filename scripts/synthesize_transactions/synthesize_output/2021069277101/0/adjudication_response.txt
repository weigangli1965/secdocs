The violations concern two main issues:

1. **Risk Profile Mismatch (Care Obligation 2(ii)(B))**:
   - The customer has a "Moderate" risk tolerance, but the transactions involve "Private placements" with a "Very High" risk profile.
   - The allocation shows 20% in Private Equity Fund (very high risk) plus additional high-risk investments like Commodities Futures (5%), resulting in approximately 30% of the portfolio in very high-risk investments.
   - According to FINRA guidance, recommendations of high-risk investments that become disproportionate to a customer's moderate risk tolerance (as low as 20% in some cases) can constitute a violation.
   - The customer's net worth ($950K) and investment goals (Wealth Growth) do not necessarily justify this level of high-risk exposure given their moderate risk tolerance.

2. **High Fees and Costs (Care Obligation 2(ii)(B))**:

     - $4,000 in fees on an $80,000 transaction (5%)
     - $2,000 in fees on a $40,000 transaction (5%)
     - $1,500 in fees on a $30,000 sale (5%)
