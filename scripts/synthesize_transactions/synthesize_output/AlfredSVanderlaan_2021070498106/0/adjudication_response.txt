# Analysis of Potential Violations under Reg BI Section (2)(ii) Care Obligation

Based on the provided information, there appears to be a potential violation of the Care Obligation under Regulation Best Interest Section (2)(ii) with respect to the recommendation of GWG L Bonds to this customer.

## Potential Violations:

### 1. Recommendation of GWG L Bonds - High Risk Investment to Senior Investor

The customer is a 72-year-old retired individual with moderate risk tolerance whose primary financial goal is retirement. Despite this profile, the broker recommended GWG L Bonds, which are explicitly classified as "high" risk investments.

The potential violations under Section (2)(ii)(B) include:

- The recommendation of a high-risk investment to a senior investor (72 years old) with moderate risk tolerance appears unsuitable considering the customer's investment profile.
- The customer's retired status, age, and retirement-focused financial goals suggest a need for more conservative investments aligned with capital preservation and income rather than high-risk growth vehicles.
- The broker should have recognized that the risk level of GWG L Bonds was inappropriate for this customer's particular investment profile, which includes advanced age, retired status, and moderate risk tolerance.

### 2. Turnover Concerns

The data shows:
- Annualized Portfolio Turnover rate of 12.5
- Cost To Equity Ratio of 2.3

While the Cost to Equity ratio of 2.3 is below the 20% threshold that would indicate excessive trading, the turnover rate of 12.5 is significantly above the 6.0 threshold mentioned in the FINRA guidance. This high turnover rate suggests potentially excessive trading in the customer's account, which could violate Section (2)(ii)(C) of the Care Obligation.

### 3. Failure to Consider the Full Investment Profile

The recommendation of GWG L Bonds suggests the broker may have failed to properly consider key aspects of the customer's investment profile, specifically:
- Age (72)
- Employment status (Retired)
- Risk tolerance (Moderate)
- Financial goals (Retirement)

## Conclusion:

There appears to be a likely violation of Reg BI's Care Obligation under Section (2)(ii), as the broker recommended a high-risk investment (GWG L Bonds) to a retirement-focused, 72-year-old customer with moderate risk tolerance. Additionally, the high portfolio turnover rate suggests that the series of transactions may have been excessive given the customer's investment profile.

The broker appears to have failed to exercise reasonable diligence, care, and skill in ensuring that recommendations were in the best interest of this particular retail customer based on their investment profile.
