No clear violations appear to exist regarding the broker's diligence in understanding the products recommended.    - Real Estate Investment Trust (5%, High risk)

2. **Very High-Risk Investments for Retirement:** The customer's financial goal is specifically listed as "Retirement," yet the broker recommended very high-risk investments like Private Equity Fund (2%) and high-risk Cryptocurrency ETF (3%). The overall portfolio allocation suggests a potential violation of the Care Obligation under section 2(ii)(B), as the broker may have recommended investments that were not in the best interest of this particular retail customer based on their moderate risk tolerance and retirement goal.
