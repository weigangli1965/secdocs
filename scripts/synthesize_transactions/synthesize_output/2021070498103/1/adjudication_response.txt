Based on the information provided, there appear to be violations of Regulation Best Interest under section (2)(ii), the Care Obligation, specifically regarding subsection (B).

The main violation concerns the recommendation of GWG L Bonds, which constitute 40% of the customer's portfolio ($262,000 out of $655,000 total investment portfolio value), despite the customer having only a "moderate" risk tolerance.

Key issues:

1. **Risk Mismatch**: The customer has a "moderate" risk tolerance, but GWG L Bonds are explicitly identified as "high" risk investments. Per FINRA guidance, recommending high-risk investments that become disproportionate to a customer with moderate risk tolerance (even as low as 20% in some cases) can constitute a violation. At 40%, the GWG L Bonds allocation significantly exceeds this threshold.

2. **Customer Profile Considerations**: The customer has a relatively modest income ($90K/year), is 55 years old (approaching retirement age), has a "moderate" risk tolerance, and has "income" as their financial goal. These factors suggest a more conservative approach would be appropriate, not concentrating 40% of their portfolio in a high-risk product.

3. **Concentration Risk**: Combined with the REIT investment (another 10% high-risk investment), the customer has 50% of their portfolio in high-risk assets despite their moderate risk tolerance. This is reflected in the "High Risk Investment Ratio" of 40% and "HighRiskNetworthRatio" of 30%, which are significant for a moderate risk investor.

4. **Significant Losses**: The customer has experienced an "Amount Lost" of $85,000, which is approximately 13% of their total portfolio value, suggesting that the recommendations may not have been in the customer's best interest.

These facts indicate that the broker or dealer failed to "have a reasonable basis to believe that the recommendation is in the best interest of a particular retail customer based on that retail customer's investment profile and the potential risks, rewards, and costs associated with the recommendation." The high concentration of high-risk investments appears inconsistent with the customer's moderate risk tolerance and income-focused financial goals.
