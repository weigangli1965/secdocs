   - Cryptocurrency ETF: 2% (High risk)

2. **Unsuitable Investment Recommendation:** The recommendation of a Cryptocurrency ETF (high-risk) appears particularly unsuitable for a 71-year-old retired investor with conservative risk tolerance and retirement goals. This recommendation does not align with the customer's investment profile and appears to place the broker's interests ahead of the customer's.

This allocation of high-risk investments does not appear to be in the best interest of this particular retail customer based on their investment profile, which violates the Care Obligation under section (2)(ii)(B). While the high-risk portion (10%) is below the 20% threshold sometimes cited by FINRA, when considered in context of this specific customer's age, retirement status, and conservative risk tolerance, these recommendations do not appear to be in the customer's best interest.
