# Violations of Reg BI - Care Obligation (2)(ii)

There appear to be violations of the Care Obligation under Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI) Section 240.15l-1(a)(2)(ii)(B), which requires that recommendations be in the best interest of the particular retail customer based on their investment profile.

## Key Violations

1. **Misalignment Between Customer Profile and Investment Recommendations**:
   - The customer has a low income ($45K/year), low net worth ($75K), and significant debt obligations (mortgage of $150K and personal loans)
   - The investment purpose is for "Anticipated upcoming expenses" (specifically home down payment or child's college tuition)
   - Despite these factors indicating a need for liquidity and capital preservation, 60% of the portfolio is allocated to high-risk investments (Technology Sector ETF 30%, REIT 15%, and Cryptocurrency ETF 15%)

2. **Unsuitable Risk Level**:
   - While the customer's risk tolerance is listed as "Aggressive," the broker must still consider the customer's overall financial situation
   - The allocation to high-risk investments is inappropriate given:
     - The customer's low income and net worth
     - The need for liquidity for anticipated upcoming expenses
     - The customer's status as a single parent with dependent children

3. **Disproportionate High-Risk Allocation**:
   - FINRA guidance notes that even for moderate risk tolerance customers, high-risk investments becoming disproportionate in a portfolio (as low as 20%) may constitute a violation
   - This customer's portfolio has 60% in high-risk investments despite having a vulnerable financial profile

4. **Mismatch Between Goals and Recommended Investments**:
   - The customer's financial goal is "Education Funding" which typically requires a more conservative approach as the time horizon approaches
   - The high allocation to volatile investments (Technology Sector ETF, Cryptocurrency ETF) is inconsistent with funds that may be needed in the near future for education expenses

The broker appears to have failed to properly consider the customer's complete investment profile when making recommendations, violating the care obligation under Reg BI. While the customer may have indicated an "Aggressive" risk tolerance, the broker still has an obligation to ensure recommendations align with the customer's overall financial situation and needs.
